Study Tips

Use these tips to build better study habits, stay organized, and manage your schoolwork more effectively.

Helpful Study Strategies

Break Tasks Into Smaller Steps

Large assignments feel easier when you divide them into smaller, manageable tasks.

Use a Weekly Plan

Plan your assignments and study sessions at the beginning of the week so deadlines do not sneak up on you.

Study Before You Are Tired

Try to study when your mind is fresh instead of waiting until the end of the day.

Review a Little Each Day

Short daily review sessions can help you remember information better than cramming all at once.

Tip of the Day

Start with the assignment that is due soonest or feels the hardest. Getting it started can make the rest of your work feel easier.
